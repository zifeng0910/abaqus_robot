## B. The alleged 11.675 ms TAIL recontact

The earlier 11.550150/11.675060 ms markers come from a regional flag using TAIL near-wall geometry **and robot whole-General-Contact nodal force**. Whole GC includes robot-CEL interaction and cannot be identified as robot-wall force. Direct robot-wall pair CFT vector magnitude is identically **0 N** throughout 11.300-11.800 ms on its native history. At nearby TAIL nodes, whole-GC force remains about 0.045-0.047 N while finite COPEN values are positive (about 0.0030-0.0040 mm) and then undefined. Therefore neither a wall-pair force pulse nor a pair-isolated wall pressure/restitution event is present at the proposed recontact.

The signed minimum gap of the actual refined TAIL nodes against the **faceted wall planes** is +3.610 um at the earlier release flag and +5.537 um at the later recontact flag; minimum between them remains +3.610 um. Over the interval, the gap increases **+1.927 um**. A two-order geometric partition of the endpoint change gives **rotation +3.093 um** (opens) and **translation -1.166 um** (partly closes), summing exactly to +1.927 um. The straight fixed tube has no varying axial wall curvature contribution. No separated then closing wall-gap trajectory is observed.

At the first flag, actual/command angles are +12.393/+9.519 deg, error +2.874 deg, omega -1688 deg/s, magnetic torque -6.540e-4 N mm. At the second, +12.128/+8.448 deg, error +3.679 deg, omega -2318 deg/s, torque -8.371e-4 N mm. Actual angle and angular velocity move **away from the positive TAIL touch threshold**, not toward it. The magnetic torque stays active and acts in the same away-from-TAIL direction. Angular momentum from 11.550150 to 11.675060 ms closes at **0.312%**: magnetic -9.311723e-8, wall 0, inferred robot-fluid +2.828656e-8, moving-RP transport -8.563e-13, measured delta H -6.445278e-8 N mm s. This is sound accounting, but does not isolate a direct fluid traction mechanism.

The regional ON/OFF toggling can be called **diagnostic-flag chatter/contamination by whole-GC load**, not a proven physical wall recontact or proven wall-penalty chatter. Because the requested alternatives presuppose a genuine wall event, the recontact-mechanism classification is inconclusive. The already-started axial reversal cannot be initiated by this later flag in any case.
